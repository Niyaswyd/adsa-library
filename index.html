About Us

Welcome to Alathurpadi Dars

The Vanguard of Classical Islamic Higher Education in Kerala

Located at the historic Alathurpadi Juma Masjid in Melmuri, Malappuram, Alathurpadi Dars stands as one of South India’s foremost traditional higher educational institutions. Established with the noble mission of preserving and propagating classical Islamic sciences, the institution provides an authentic, rigorous, and completely free residential scholarly education.

Under the visionary leadership of eminent scholar Dr. C.K. Muhammad Abdurahman Faizy Aripra (Central Mushawara Member, Samastha Kerala Jam’iyyathul Ulama), Alathurpadi Dars integrates classical Dars Nizam pedagogy with contemporary research, linguistic eloquence, and IT competence.

Comprehensive Learning Integrating Classical Islamic Sciences, Humanities, Modern Technology, and Life Skills within a single learning ecosystem.
Actionable Knowledge Translating academic research and literature into real-world applications through practical exposure and scholarly mentorship.
